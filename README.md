# CodZombiesTips

Back in 2015, I created my very first website. At school, I took a course called Informatica, where I learned the basics of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. For my final assignment, I had to create a website about a subject that interested me. So, being a game-obsessed 15-year-old, I chose my favorite game: Call of Duty: Zombies.

And so, CodZombieTips.neocities.org was born. It’s fun to look back at it now, especially since both I and the tech world as a whole have come a long way since then.

I uploaded this site so everyone could see that I started somewhere too, before reaching the level I’m at today. I hope it inspires you to reflect on how far you’ve come and helps ease your imposter syndrome a little. You probably started out with humble website roots like these, too.

It’s been 8 years since I created this website, and I’m really looking forward to seeing what I’ll create over the next 8 years!
